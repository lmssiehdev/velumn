## No channels are showing up on the dashboard

If you don't see any channels on the dashboard, it's likely because the bot doesn't have the permissions to see them. If the channels are hidden behind a role, give it to the bot.

The bot requires all permissions to work as intended. If you changed them when inviting the bot, reinvite it again with full permissions enabled.

## My server's threads aren't showing up on Google

If you see threads on Velumn but not on Google, don't worry this is normal. We let Google know about the threads using a sitemap and follow best SEO practices, but we have no control over what Google indexes.

Google crawls and indexes content on its own schedule, which can take anywhere from a few days to several weeks. If it's been over a month and your content still hasn't appeared in search results, contact us.

## Formatting looks wrong in forum posts

We are slowly expanding our support and plan to match every Discord feature, If something looks wrong, notify us and we will fix it immediately.

## Messages are missing or partially indexed

The bot is designed to resume indexing from where it left off and automatically retries failed messages, so missing messages should be rare. If you encounter this issue, you can force reindexing by running the `/index-thread` command inside the Discord thread that needs to be reindexed.
